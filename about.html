About This Site

I built this site as a place to put my projects in one spot instead of scattering them across different repos and hackathon devposts. I'm a computer science student, and most of what I do outside of class is build things fast under a deadline, usually at a hackathon with a team of two or three people and way too little sleep.

I got motivated to actually make a real site for this after building a few projects I was proud of and realizing nobody could see them unless I sent a direct link. I wanted something simple that shows who I am, what I've worked on, and how to reach me.

I'm still adding to it as I go, so expect this to change as I build more.
